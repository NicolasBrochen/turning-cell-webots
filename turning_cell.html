3D animation: Robotic turning cell

Robotic turning cell (Webots R2025a): two CNC lathes facing each other, tended by a UR10e with a dual SCHUNK-type gripper on a 90° bent bracket and a 6-axis force/torque sensor.

Blanks from a crate, unload + load of each lathe in a single visit (dual gripper), force-guarded seating in the chuck, turning cycle, finished parts to the output crate, crate exchange by an operator with laser scanners slowing then stopping the robot.
Left click: orbit · right click: pan · mouse wheel: zoom · bottom bar: play / scrub the timeline.

Source code: github.com/NicolasBrochen/turning-cell-webots
